- **Rebranding**: taking this work, changing the name, the logo or the icon, and
  presenting the result as your own product. This is the case the licence and

- Offering Skales, or anything derived from it, as a **hosted or managed service**,

## How originals identify themselves

Published builds are signed, and every build carries an identity of its own and
presents it to Skales' own services. A build that does not appear in our own
release record is not one of ours, whatever it is called. We use that to tell a
copy from an original; it is not a restriction on you, and it says nothing about
who is using a machine.
